01 / Problem

Most teams aren’t short on ideas. They’re short on contact with reality.

Founders are paid to believe. That’s useful until it becomes a distortion field: a roadmap written in a conference room, defended with vanity metrics, while the funnel quietly dies. The lab exists to cut through that.

  1. FM-01

    The distortion field

    A team can talk anyone into a roadmap, including itself. That’s part of building a company. It is also how a year disappears.

  2. FM-02

    Tunnel vision

    You’re too close to the product to see the drop-off. Users stall. Funnels leak. The next feature ships anyway.

  3. FM-03

    Vanity in the dashboard

    Signups, impressions, a graph for the board. None of it answers the only question that matters: what should we build next?
